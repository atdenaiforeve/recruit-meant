There are 28 development roles across seven departments. The supplied numbering runs from 1 to 29, with no role listed at number 3. Open a role to see what it involves, whether a computer is needed, and what you can use to get started. Some design roles can begin with paper and pencil even if a computer is needed later to create the finished game work. Roles may be shared or combined where that makes sense for the team's size and skills.

27. QA Tester

Computer needed: Not required if you have another supported device that can run the game; a computer is helpful for detailed reports.
