About the game

Beyond the Horizon is a planned open-world survival and exploration game on Roblox. The world is a large archipelago made up of major islands, smaller island chains and open ocean.

The project is being developed in stages. The first stage focuses on creating the world's natural physical geography. Environments, creatures and gameplay systems will be developed later.

Important information

All positions are unpaid and voluntary. This is a volunteer project, not paid employment. Only apply if you understand and are comfortable with that.

The game is being made in Roblox Studio. Developers need access to a computer that can run Roblox Studio and should be ready to work collaboratively.

Clear communication, respect, realistic time commitments and willingness to learn are important.
