Beyond the Horizon

A large-scale open-world survival game set across a vast archipelago. We are building a team to help bring its world and gameplay to life.

About the project

Beyond the Horizon is a survival and exploration game planned for Roblox. Players will explore a connected world of large islands, smaller island chains and open ocean.

The project is being developed in stages, starting with the world's physical geography before moving on to environments, creatures and gameplay systems.

Team roles

We are looking for people with skills and interest in areas such as:

Project Manager

Help organise tasks, progress and team communication.

Lead Programmer

Guide the game's technical systems and code structure.

Gameplay Programmer

Build gameplay features and interactive systems.

Terrain Designer

Create natural landforms and large-scale terrain.

World Builder

Build locations, structures and points of interest.

3D Artist

Create models and visual assets for the game.

Environment Artist

Develop the look and atmosphere of locations.

Animator

Create animations for players, animals, tools and interactions.

UI Designer

Design menus, HUD elements and player interfaces.

Sound Designer

Create and organise environmental and gameplay audio.

Game Designer

Help plan gameplay mechanics, systems and progression.

Three playtesters have already been identified. Development roles may be combined or shared depending on the team's skills.

Working together

We are looking for team members who communicate clearly, work respectfully with others, can show examples of their skills and are realistic about the time they can contribute.

People should apply for roles that match their actual interests and abilities. You do not need to be an expert, but you should be willing to learn and work reliably.

Volunteer development team

All positions are unpaid and voluntary. This is a collaborative project, not paid employment. By applying, you confirm that you understand and are comfortable with the unpaid arrangement.

Team members are expected to contribute respectfully, communicate clearly and agree on realistic tasks and time commitments.
