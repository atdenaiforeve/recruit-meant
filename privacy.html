Recruitment information · Pre-launch draft

Privacy and applicant safety

This is a draft, not a final privacy policy. Applications are not open. A parent or guardian must review and approve the data-handling arrangements and complete the missing details before any real applications are collected.

Information the preview form asks for

  • A chosen display name and broad age range.
  • The role selected and tools the applicant uses.
  • A short explanation of why the applicant wants the role and the skills or experience they would bring.
  • A preferred contact method (email, Discord or another method) and the contact detail needed for that method.

The form must not request passwords, home addresses, phone numbers, exact birth dates, school details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Why information would be collected

If recruitment is approved, information would only be used to review the volunteer application, understand the applicant's skills and contact them about that application.

Who could access it

Application records are designed to be stored in the project's Cloudflare D1 database. Access should be limited to the authorised project administrator and an approved parent or guardian/supervisor. Information must not be posted publicly, shared with other applicants or used for unrelated purposes.

Storage and deletion — must be confirmed before launch

Before applications open, the project administrator and parent/guardian must decide and publish:

  • How long unsuccessful and successful applications will be kept.
  • How an applicant or their parent/guardian can request access, correction or deletion.
  • Who is responsible for handling those requests and how they can be contacted.
  • How access tokens are protected and who is authorised to use the dashboard.

Launch condition: Do not enable application intake until these details are completed, the form and dashboard have been tested with dummy data, and a parent or guardian has reviewed the full process.
