What every developer needs

  • Access to a computer that can run Roblox Studio.
  • Willingness to learn and improve in your chosen area.
  • Ability to communicate clearly with the team.
  • Respect for other team members and their work.
  • A realistic idea of how much time you can contribute.
  • Ability to follow agreed tasks, ask for help and explain problems.

Experience

Applicants should be honest about their current skill level. Previous Roblox Studio projects or examples of work are useful, but role-specific experience will be considered on the role page.

You do not need to know every part of Roblox Studio. Apply for a role that matches what you can do or are actively learning.

Tools, assets and approval

Developers may use suitable creative and development tools for their role. The tools below are examples, not a mandatory list.

  • Roblox Studio: build, test and prepare content for the game.
  • Blender: create and edit 3D models, props and other suitable assets.
  • Image and audio tools: create or edit suitable textures, interface graphics and sound assets.
  • Other tools: you may suggest another tool if it is relevant to your role.

How asset approval works

  1. Create the asset using a suitable tool, such as making a model in Blender.
  2. Import or upload it to Roblox in a test or draft workflow, if needed.
  3. Show the asset to a project lead or other authorised senior team member.
  4. Wait for approval before adding it to the official game or main project.

Important: Uploading an asset to Roblox does not mean it is approved for Beyond the Horizon. Do not add, publish or replace official game content without permission from an authorised project lead. Keep original project files and only use assets you created or have permission to use.
