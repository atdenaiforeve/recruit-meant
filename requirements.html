What every developer needs

  • Access to a computer that can run Roblox Studio.
  • Willingness to learn and improve in your chosen area.
  • Ability to communicate clearly with the team.
  • Respect for other team members and their work.
  • A realistic idea of how much time you can contribute.
  • Ability to follow agreed tasks, ask for help and explain problems.

Experience

Applicants should be honest about their current skill level. Previous Roblox Studio projects or examples of work are useful, but role-specific experience will be considered on the role page.

You do not need to know every part of Roblox Studio. Apply for a role that matches what you can do or are actively learning.
